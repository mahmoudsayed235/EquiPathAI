# EquiPath AI — Junction 2026 Submission

**Project Name:** EquiPath AI

**Punchline:** Turning cancer care data into actionable insights for more equitable cancer care across Finland.

**Challenge:** Equal Care Across Finland — AstraZeneca

---

## Overview

Where you live should never determine the quality or timeliness of your cancer care.

Yet across Finland, differences in healthcare access, diagnostic capacity, and treatment pathways can create unequal experiences for cancer patients.

EquiPath AI is an AI-powered healthcare intelligence platform designed to uncover regional inequalities, identify potential bottlenecks, and help decision-makers improve cancer care pathways using evidence-driven insights.

By combining publicly available Finnish cancer statistics, healthcare performance indicators, and AI-powered analysis, EquiPath AI transforms fragmented healthcare data into a clearer picture of where improvements are needed most.

## How It Works

Starting with lung cancer as our initial use case, EquiPath AI examines four critical stages of the cancer care journey:

**Early Detection:** Identify regional variations in cancer incidence, survival, mortality, and available early-detection indicators.

**Referral:** Analyze healthcare accessibility and available waiting-time indicators to uncover potential delays.

**Diagnosis:** Investigate diagnostic access, available staging indicators, and gaps in regional reporting.

**Treatment Initiation:** Evaluate available treatment access and waiting-time statistics to highlight potential inequalities.

The platform presents these insights through an interactive regional dashboard, allowing healthcare leaders to explore differences across Finland's wellbeing services counties.

## AI-Powered Intelligence

EquiPath AI introduces four intelligent capabilities:

**Equity Mapping:** Visualize and compare regional cancer care indicators, highlighting significant differences between healthcare regions.

**Bottleneck Intelligence:** Identify potential weaknesses along the cancer care pathway and explain their possible implications.

**Actionable AI Recommendations:** Suggest evidence-backed interventions aligned with Finland's National Cancer Strategy 2026–2035.

**Scenario Exploration:** Simulate potential improvements from hypothetical interventions, helping decision-makers compare priorities before committing resources.

An integrated conversational AI analyst enables users to ask questions such as:

"Which Finnish regions show the greatest potential gaps in cancer care access, and what interventions should be investigated first?"

The AI responds with explainable findings, supporting evidence, and practical recommendations.

## Built on Real Finnish Healthcare Data

EquiPath AI is designed to integrate publicly available information from:

- Finnish Cancer Registry
- THL Sotkanet
- Public healthcare waiting-time statistics
- Finland's National Cancer Strategy 2026–2035

Data transparency is fundamental to our approach.

The platform clearly separates observed statistics, AI-generated interpretations, and hypothetical simulations. When cancer-specific pathway data is missing, the system identifies the limitation instead of inventing conclusions.

## Expected Impact

EquiPath AI aims to help Finnish healthcare organizations:

- Detect regional cancer care inequalities earlier.
- Identify and prioritize healthcare pathway improvements.
- Support more transparent, evidence-based resource allocation.
- Translate national cancer policy into measurable regional actions.
- Monitor progress toward more equitable cancer care.

Our ambition is not simply to build another healthcare dashboard, but to create an intelligent decision-support system that helps turn complex healthcare evidence into meaningful action.

**Our vision: Better cancer care shouldn't depend on your postcode. EquiPath AI helps Finland see the gaps, understand the causes, and prioritize the path toward equitable care.**
